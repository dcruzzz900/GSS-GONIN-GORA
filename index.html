Common questions

What are the school hours?

The school runs two shifts from Monday to Thursday, and every period is 30 minutes. The morning shift starts at 7:30 with the day’s assembly, lessons run from 8:00 to 12:30, and there is a 30-minute break at 10:00. The afternoon shift arrives at 12:30 for the day’s assembly, lessons start at 13:00 and end at 17:30, and there is a 30-minute break at 15:00.

Which assemblies does the school hold each week?

Monday: general assembly, then staff briefing. Tuesday: gender assembly. Wednesday: assembly, then staff briefing. Thursday: house assembly. Both shifts follow this pattern before lessons begin.

Which classes does the school offer?

The school is a senior secondary school, teaching SS1 to SS3. Each year group has five classes: Science 1, Science 2, Art 1, Art 2 and Commercial.

How do I apply for admission?

Fill in the form on this page. The school office will contact you about the next steps and the documents you need.

Can I visit the school first?

Yes. Contact the school office to arrange a visit.

Who do I contact about my child’s attendance or results?

Start with the class teacher, then the school office if you need more help.
